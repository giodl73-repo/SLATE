## Interpretation and source reuse

[NCES's ratio explanation](https://ies.ed.gov/learn/blog/changes-pupilteacher-ratios-2020-impacts-covid-19-pandemic)
distinguishes pupil/teacher ratios from average class size. This task does not
evaluate instructional workload or quality. Published integer FTE counts retain
table precision and do not establish teacher headcounts or individual staffing.

The frozen official statistical table text/data are reproduced with attribution;
no images or external resources are included in the task. Original source notices
remain in the HTML and [SOURCE-NOTICE](nces/README.md) describes custody and limits.
SLATE's original content/code licenses do not change third-party source rights.

All figures in the fictional Cedar graph remain simulated. None is calibrated
against this source slice. Source rows do not create or promote a scored corpus
entry, source-to-service claim, intervention or fiscal handoff.
