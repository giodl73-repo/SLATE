# Student / Family

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the student or household trying to get quality schooling when geography, cost, language, disability, transportation, admissions, assignment, or time off work may block access.

## Primary concerns

- Timely, nearby quality schooling and seat availability (DIM-01).
- Affordability, transportation, fees, tuition, and hidden family cost (DIM-07).
- Feeder, transfer, and articulation continuity without falling through handoffs (DIM-04).

## Used during

- Access, affordability, and pathway-continuity scoring; tier/SLA for primary, secondary, specialized, and postsecondary access.
