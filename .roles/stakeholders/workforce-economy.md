# Workforce / Economy

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures employers, regional economies, workforce boards, and communities that depend on attainment, credentials, and skill pathways.

## Primary concerns

- Credential, CTE, postsecondary, and skill-pipeline continuity (DIM-04/08).
- Population attainment, completion, career readiness, and mobility (DIM-10/03).
- Whether public investment builds durable workforce value (DIM-12).

## Used during

- Program-breadth, pathway, mobility-impact, and benefit-cost scoring; tier/SLA review for secondary, postsecondary, CTE, and specialized pathways.
