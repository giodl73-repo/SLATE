# Frontline Educator

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures the teachers, counselors, aides, and education teams who actually deliver instruction, advising, student supports, and program continuity.

## Primary concerns

- Workload, staffing, retention, burnout, and ability to teach well (DIM-06/03).
- Whether capacity is staffed and deliverable at reasonable class size (DIM-02).
- Handoff quality, advising, transfer/articulation closure, and instructional risk (DIM-04).

## Used during

- Workforce, quality, capacity, and pathway scoring; tier/SLA feasibility review with the educator instruction lead.
