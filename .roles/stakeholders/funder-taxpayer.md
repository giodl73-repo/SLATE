# Funder / Taxpayer

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures taxpayers, public funders, philanthropies, and finance officers concerned with per-pupil cost, capital cost, operating sustainability, and equity of funding.

## Primary concerns

- Per-pupil cost, capital cost, operating cost, and family affordability (DIM-07).
- Benefit-cost and value of spend (DIM-12).
- Funding equity, formula constraints, program breadth, and boundary/governance feasibility (DIM-11/08).

## Used during

- Affordability, benefit-cost, funding/governance, and program-breadth scoring; tier/SLA feasibility review with the funding and governance realist.
