# Rural / Underserved Community

A lens, not a reviewer. Captures communities where school closure, consolidation, thin staffing, specialized-program deserts, transportation barriers, broadband/device gaps, or funding formulas can make access fragile or absent.

## Primary concerns

- Having any local or reachable quality access at all (DIM-01/11).
- Teacher shortages, program closure, consolidation, and transfer dependence (DIM-06/08/04).
- Affordability, transportation, and funding barriers (DIM-07).

## Used during

- Equity, access, workforce, program-breadth, and resilience scoring; tier/SLA for local and regional access (T3/T4).
