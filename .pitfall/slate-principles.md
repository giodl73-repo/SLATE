# SLATE Principles

## SLATE-P-01: Seat Presence Is Not Education Access

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** A seat is not access if the student cannot reach, enter, afford,
or progress through it.

**Rationale:** Education capacity is non-fungible across grade, program,
language, support, pathway, governance, and affordability constraints.

**Decision rule:** Promoted findings must keep access, capacity, workforce,
program, pathway, affordability, outcomes, resilience, scale, and demand basis
separate.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/vtrace/REQUIREMENTS.md`, and
`docs/vtrace/SPECIFICATION_BASELINE.md`.

## SLATE-P-02: Aggregate Evidence Only Until Public Source Review

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** SLATE uses aggregate and synthetic evidence unless a public, safe,
source-backed corpus has explicit review.

**Rationale:** Education data can implicate student privacy, assignment,
funding, labor, and governance decisions.

**Decision rule:** No student records or individual educational recommendations
enter the repo; public corpus work needs source, privacy, governance, and role
review.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `PRODUCT_PLAN.md`, `docs/adoption/README.md`, and
`docs/vtrace/REVIEW.md`.

## SLATE-P-03: Transfer Strain Is A Finding, Not A Failure

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Dimensions that do not transfer cleanly from physical lifelines or
SHIELD's healthcare stress test must be reported, not forced.

**Rationale:** Non-conserved relational edges, single-score fairness, and
non-fungible capacity recur in education and require calibration discipline.

**Decision rule:** Calibration must preserve service-network transfer-strain
findings and either resolve or carry them explicitly.

**Evidence:** `docs/vtrace/REVIEW.md`, `docs/vtrace/TRACE.md`, and
`docs/vtrace/SPECIFICATION_BASELINE.md`.

## SLATE-P-04: Scale And Demand Basis Bind Every Claim

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** Scores, tiers, gaps, and concepts are interpreted only at the
declared scale and demand basis.

**Rationale:** International, national, regional, and local education claims use
different denominators, governance surfaces, and intervention meanings.

**Decision rule:** Missing scale or demand basis holds promotion; cross-scale
comparisons require explicit markers.

**Evidence:** `CLAUDE.md`, `docs/vtrace/VERIFICATION.md`,
`crates/slate-corpus/src/lib.rs`, and `crates/slate-gap/src/lib.rs`.

## SLATE-P-05: Research Output Is Not Education Authority

**Status:** ACTIVE

**Statement:** SLATE is not student advice, accreditation, licensing,
assignment, boundary, funding, charter, voucher, labor-contract, curriculum, or
institutional endorsement authority.

**Rationale:** Education evidence and education decisions have different legal,
governance, professional, and community accountability owners.

**Decision rule:** Public-facing outputs must retain research-lab framing,
aggregate/source limits, and no-authority language before reuse or publication.

**Evidence:** `README.md`, `PRODUCT_PLAN.md`, `.roles/ROLE.md`, and
`docs/vtrace/REVIEW.md`.
