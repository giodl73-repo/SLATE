# SLATE Open Adoption Guide

## Purpose

SLATE is public and open to use as a reference model for aggregate,
evidence-gated education-access analysis. It tests where service-network methods
transfer to education delivery and where they break.

Public use does not create a pedagogical study, accreditation or licensing
determination, funding or assignment decision, student-level advice, advocacy
brief, or district, state, ministry, board, institution, or funder endorsement.

## Safe Adaptation Path

Use [`aggregate-adaptation-worksheet.md`](aggregate-adaptation-worksheet.md) to
scope one aggregate-only seat, educator, program, pathway, affordability,
continuity, equity, or resilience question.

Do not use student records. Do not produce individual educational advice. Do not
turn transfer-strain evidence into an assignment, funding, or pedagogy
recommendation.

## Contribution Targets

- aggregate source inventories for schools, institutions, programs, educators,
  boundaries, pathways, affordability, or resilience evidence;
- privacy and public-language review;
- corrections to capacity, pathway, equity, affordability, or continuity claims;
- reviewer notes from education planning, district operations, workforce,
  access, equity, accreditation, finance, or public-language lenses.

## Gate

Decision: **open_for_aggregate_reference_review**

Rationale: SLATE can accept bounded aggregate source and review contributions.
Reuse alone does not create pedagogy, accreditation, assignment, funding,
student-advice, endorsement, or validation claims.
